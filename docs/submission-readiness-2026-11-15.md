## Organizer requirements and template

The [2026 participant terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/) require all sources used in the project to be cited and applicable licenses for third-party materials to be identified in submission documentation. They also set terms for availability and use of original and third-party content. A [Global Organizing Team answer](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1849) says to disclose where and how AI was used, with visible watermarks for AI-generated images and video and acknowledgment in descriptive text and metadata for AI-generated audio, code and data. It also identifies a challenge-listed NASA data source as a Global Awards requirement. The organizer's [submission-format answer](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1858) lists a project title, summary, demo and project links, detailed description, data resources, AI/SI tool use and agreement to terms.

The official [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) could not be fetched during this review. The six-point README template supplied by the owner guided this revision; its relationship to that official guide has not been independently verified. The [2026 event page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/) gives a November 15, 11:59 p.m. local deadline; the [organizing team](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1916) says the assigned Local Event time zone of the team owner controls.

## Data, software and media attribution

- The [source inventory](data-sources.md), [research collection](../data/nasa-research/README.md) and collection metadata trace NASA records, publications, source captures and table locations. Rights assertions are source-specific. PSI-10 has no recorded license identifier, and NASA/NTRS assets and individual linked materials cannot be assumed to inherit a record-level CC0 assertion.
- The [third-party software inventory](third-party-software-2026-11-15.md) lists locked Python and JavaScript packages, versions, declared licenses and runtime/development reachability, and identifies bundled asset notices and container/runtime review work. Declared metadata alone does not satisfy every distribution obligation. Inspect the actual release, package license files, native components and images, and retain applicable notices.
- The [video credits](../media/nova-ember-space-apps/credits.md) name the NASA media, research records, music and modifications. Attribution and the CC BY 4.0 license link for the music need to accompany the actual video wherever it is shared. Verify the final media and public page, not just the local credits file.
- The owner supplied the source image (SHA-256 `05ac55af84de3e602c8c4108cf344f37233fe8dc4865e607803eee4144759286`) and reports it was upscaled using ChatGPT Images 2.0. Google Lens returned exact visual matches including [Frock Flicks’ 2015 Troy article](https://frockflicks.com/mcm-eric-bana/) and a Fandom page. Those pages do not establish the original creator, a Creative Commons license for the film image, or permission to modify and redistribute it.
