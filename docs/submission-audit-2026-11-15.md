Examples include the [BASS-II image and FLEX videos](../tests/fixtures/nasa-psi-media/snapshot.json) and [FLEX and Saffire method documents](../tests/fixtures/nasa-psi-methods/snapshot.json). Review original notices and rights for each asset before relying on blanket reuse permission, especially for PSI-10 and third-party content.
