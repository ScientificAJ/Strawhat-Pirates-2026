# Nova Ember demonstration and submission guide

Prepared 15 November 2026. This is a reviewable demonstration outline and submission preparation document. It does not record a submission, a deployed URL, new user studies or scientific certification. Replace pending release facts with observed evidence before publishing.

## Product description

**Nova Ember turns NASA combustion research into a question-led scientific workflow.** People can find investigations, understand their purpose, compare experimental conditions, inspect the original evidence and work with source-bound data. The research assistant connects explanations and scientific tools to inspectable sources. Automatic continuity helps visitors return to research without first managing a workspace or creating a case.

The project addresses the NASA Space Apps [Flame in Freefall challenge](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/). It is developed by team **StrawHat Pirates**.

## A short demonstration

Demonstrate one complete question in depth before touring additional tools. Use the application revision and source corpus named in the verification report; show the deployed origin only after it has been checked.

1. Start on the home page. Explain that this is a way to explore research about fire in microgravity. Ask: **“Which NASA investigations could help me understand how ventilation affects flame spread in solid materials?”** Show the actual submitted question and progress. If a live model is unavailable, say so and complete the source-browsing path instead of displaying a fixture as a real answer.
2. Follow a cited or searched investigation such as **BASS-II**. Read the study's objective in plain language, then inspect an original table or supporting source. Show that a study objective and an observed result have different meanings.
3. Select a second relevant investigation, such as a Saffire study, and open comparison. Explain at least one real condition or scale difference. Do not imply that two selected studies are quantitatively interchangeable.
4. Open retained data from the selected study. Inspect a value and its original source location, then chart or export an actual selection. State whether the view contains measured values, source-reported settings or a derived result.
5. Return to the saved question or analysis to show automatic continuity. Explain portable backup only if demonstrating moving or protecting research.

If time permits, add one separate calculation or modeling example. A simple independently checked control is **21% oxygen by mole at 100 kPa absolute → 21 kPa oxygen partial pressure**. Explain that this calculation does not establish equal flammability across different atmospheres. For fitting, use the registered calibrated trace path and show its input/interval requirements. Do not call an idealized D² visualization an observed reconstruction or a validated predictor.

## Challenge objectives to show with evidence

| Objective | What to demonstrate | What not to imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summarize and interpret | Source-linked explanation and opening its recorded source location | A citation alone proves every scientific interpretation |
| Rank relevant evidence | Explicit research criteria, supporting assessments and inspectable ordering when available | Alphabetical results, similarity or an unsupported score are a fire-safety ranking |
| Compare investigations | Purpose, conditions, endpoints and meaningful differences | Matching titles or labels establish independent, poolable experiments |
| Support data extraction/modeling | Actual source selection, units, transformations, chart/export or calibrated descriptive fit | A successful fit establishes prediction outside the observed data |
| Inform controls/equipment research | Relevant mechanism/apparatus evidence plus remaining validation needs | Equipment sizing, certification or operational safety has been established |
| Consider Earth applications | Source-described prospective use and condition/transfer differences | Microgravity results transfer automatically to an Earth setting |

## Before submitting

- The official challenge Details and Resources were read in the browser on 15 November 2026; the [product brief records the verified list](../MASTER_BRIEF.md#communication-references). Recheck it for later additions before submission. The live PSI list returned 26 records while the retained corpus contains 24; do not claim the additional studies have been integrated until their source release is actually reviewed.
- Check the current submission form, eligibility, deadline and organizer rules through official sources. The old brief's legal/disclosure summaries are historical, not a substitute for the governing requirements at submission time.
- Verify every visible scientific number, chart label and source in the recording against the final app and retained evidence. Use actual provider output or identify its absence; synthetic interface fixtures are not research results.
- Inspect the public URL in a fresh browser. Run the meaningful source, comparison, data/export and assistant paths; confirm denied access to another visitor's private tasks.
- Keep secrets and private receipts out of screenshots, recordings, logs and submission assets. Use the project's own visual identity and preserve accurate data/media credits.
- Make the final description match the delivered behavior. Prepared assets, a successful push, a deployment and an actual submitted project are separate states.
