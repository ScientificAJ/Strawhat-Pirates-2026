## Visual sources

- **Candle flames in normal gravity and microgravity**, NASA Glenn Research Center, image GRC-1998-C-00486. [NASA image page](https://images.nasa.gov/details/GRC-1998-C-00486); [item-specific public-domain information](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Space_Fire.jpg). The normal-gravity side is cropped and held as a still; it is a candle, not a spacecraft cabin test.
- **Great Balls of Fire — FLEX**, Science@NASA and NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center. [NASA Scientific Visualization Studio](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/11318/); [NASA SVS reuse information](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/). The footage was trimmed, enlarged, and used without its original audio. SVS states its content is public domain unless otherwise noted.
- **Nova Ember product screens** were captured from the local application using retained NASA source material, including PSI-69 FLEX and PSI-25 BASS-II. The source drawer shows source metadata, not live telemetry.
