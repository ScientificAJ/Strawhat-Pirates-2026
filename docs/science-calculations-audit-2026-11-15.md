# Scientific calculations review — 2026-11-15

This is a bounded code and reference review of the dashboard's registered calculations. It checks definitions, units, numerical boundaries and stated uncertainty against the implementation. It does **not** establish comprehensive scientific validation, validate source measurements or calibrations, reproduce NASA experiments, or certify prediction or safety performance.

## Coverage and scientific contract

Reviewed `packages/analysis` (`oxygen`, `derived`, `events`, `fits`, `media`, `uncertainty`, `visualization`), the `ConditionValue` comparison boundary, the shared scientific input/result contracts, and `api/methods.py`. The OLS evaluation and prediction formulas in `packages/modeling/regression.py` were inspected as dependencies of the method dispatcher. Source normalization, evidence verification, ranking policy and rendered dashboard values have separate review ownership; they are not independently certified by this report.

## Findings corrected

1. **Unresolved prose could become a matched scientific condition.** `ConditionValue(status="nominal", value="unknown", source_locator=...)` previously passed validation. Identical placeholders could therefore satisfy a comparison rule and contribute to pooling eligibility. Known statuses now reject recognized missing/unresolved prose, including spaced or underscore variants. Callers must retain explicit `not_reported`/`unresolved` status. Numeric zero remains valid. This does not authenticate arbitrary user-supplied condition values or references.

2. **Normal-denominator Monte Carlo could claim stable moments without a defensible moment model.** The generic ratio could return finite mean/std and `converged=True` from a finite draw although a stochastic Gaussian denominator generally produces undefined ratio moments. The same denominator problem occurs for Gaussian temperature in oxygen molar density. These moments-based paths now require a fixed nonzero denominator; fixed positive temperature remains supported. [Marsaglia's original normal-ratio analysis](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v016i04) explains the moment problem. This is a conservative supported-domain restriction: special exact cancellation cases are not separately recognized. No truncated/lognormal distribution or tail threshold is invented. A justified bounded-distribution method would be additional work.

3. **Detector component arithmetic could reject an exact decimal balance.** A declared total of 0.3 s with components 0.1 s and 0.2 s failed because the binary sum slightly exceeded the total. Components now use compensated summation; only a small bound derived from floating-point spacing permits a rounding-level negative residual to become zero. This is not a measurement-uncertainty allowance. A material excess, any positive component against a zero total, and nonfinite totals are still refused.

4. **Positive partial pressure could silently become zero at underflow.** A positive composition/pressure combination outside representable output range now fails explicitly. Genuine zero oxygen remains a valid zero. No pressure conversion, rounding or fabricated replacement value is introduced.

5. **A positive pixel diameter could silently become a zero metre diameter.** Calibration now refuses this underflow rather than returning a zero-sized measured object. Signed front coordinates may still legitimately be zero.

6. **The grayscale channel needed an explicit radiometric qualification.** The legacy `luminance_bt601` contract is preserved for compatibility, with a returned limitation explaining that it is an 8-bit luma-weighted grayscale transform, not calibrated radiometric luminance. Color/intensity still supplies no temperature inference.

## Limits and remaining conditions

Most listed formulas were reviewed without a fresh test of every parameter combination. The review did not establish source calibration quality, physical independence of the selected records, representativeness, residual normality, covariance provenance, correctness of a caller's Jacobian, or completeness of a supplied chemical/flow model. Reference strings and declarations remain inputs requiring source inspection.

Normal-distribution approximations for bounded composition and positive pressure require a defensible application-specific justification. Refusing invalid draws does not establish that an entire normal distribution has physical support. The fixed-denominator restriction prevents a specific unsupported moment summary; it does not turn the remaining distribution choices into validated measurement uncertainty. No unreported calibration uncertainty, systematic error, model discrepancy, confidence percentage or empirical agreement was added.
